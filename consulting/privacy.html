Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

1. Introduction

QVXX Ltd ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our website and services.

QVXX Ltd (company number 16612070, registered at Flat 3 Misterton Court, Westbridge Road, London, England, SW11 3NL) is the controller of the personal information we use to run our own business. This includes website, enquiry, booking, client contact and billing records. When a client asks us to process personal information for it, that client is the controller. We act only on its documented instructions, under a written agreement. Please send questions about that processing to the client.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide to us:

  • Contact information (name, email address, phone number) when you schedule consultations or contact us
  • Scheduling information when you book consultations via Google Calendar
  • Professional information when you engage our services
  • Communication records when you correspond with us

2.2 Information collected automatically:

  • Technical data (IP address, browser type, device information)
  • Usage data (pages visited, time spent on site) via Cloudflare Web Analytics and Google Analytics
  • Cookies and similar technologies (see our Cookie Policy)

2.3 Information from others:

Business contact details of client staff, given to us by their employer, and personal information contained in project data that a client supplies for agreed work.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data to:

  • Provide our AI consulting services
  • Respond to your enquiries and schedule consultations
  • Send service-related communications
  • Improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations

We may use AI services to help with these purposes, and we share only what each task needs. We do not use personal information to train or develop AI models for our own business purposes. If a client commissions us to train a model, we do so only on that client's documented instructions, after the client has confirmed that it has the necessary permissions and a lawful basis.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data based on:

  • Contract: where you are personally our client, or you asked us to take steps before entering into a contract with you.
  • Legitimate interests: responding to enquiries, managing relationships with client organisations and their staff, delivering agreed work, improving our website, and establishing or defending legal claims. We weigh these interests against your rights and interests.
  • Consent: non-essential cookies and analytics, and marketing where the law requires consent.
  • Legal obligation: accounting, tax and record keeping.

When we process personal information on a client's behalf, we follow the client's instructions and the client decides the lawful basis.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information with:

  • Service providers acting on our instructions under written terms, including:
    • Google Workspace (email, calendar and storage)
    • Cloudflare (hosting and web analytics)
    • Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager
    • OpenAI (ChatGPT and API) and Anthropic (Claude and API) for AI-assisted analysis and drafting
    • Miro (collaboration)
    Each receives only what its task needs.
  • Professional advisers (lawyers, accountants) when necessary
  • Law enforcement or regulatory bodies when legally required

6. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as its purpose requires:

  • Enquiries and business administration: while we handle the enquiry or relationship. We delete it once it is no longer needed.
  • Contracts, invoices and related records: as long as tax and accounting law requires, and while a legal claim could reasonably be brought.
  • Website analytics: according to the retention settings in our analytics tools.
  • Marketing: until you unsubscribe or object.

We keep, return or delete information we process for a client as that client instructs. When we delete information, removal from our service providers' systems follows their own deletion processes.

7. Your Rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent (where applicable)
  • Object at any time to direct marketing

If a decision with legal or similarly significant effects on you is made solely by automated means, you can ask for information about it, make representations, ask for human intervention, and contest the decision. Stricter limits apply where special category data is involved.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@qvxx.ai.

8. International Transfers

Some service providers may process personal information outside the UK. We transfer it only where UK data protection law allows.

9. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
