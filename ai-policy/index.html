AI and client information

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · Version 1.0

We use AI throughout our consulting work to research, analyse information, develop software and draft documents more quickly. We make the professional judgements, check the results and take responsibility for what we deliver.

QVXX is led by Natanael Mota, who built Vortex Files with AI-assisted development, including its client access and file-permission controls. His experience includes product and messaging platforms in regulated financial services at Remitly and Checkout.com. At Remitly, he used AI-assisted analysis to identify substantial potential annual savings in SMS costs.

Our tools include ChatGPT and the OpenAI API, Claude and the Anthropic API, and Miro. We can tell you which of them we would use for your work.

Client information

We may use real client information in systems and AI service accounts that QVXX operates, where our agreement with the client and the law allow it. We treat that information as confidential, use only what the work needs and do not use one client's information in work for another. Where a client restricts particular information or services, we follow that restriction.

Training AI models

We do not use client information to train or fine-tune AI models, and we do not allow providers to use it to improve theirs, unless the client has separately authorised that use and it is lawful.

Personal data

Where we process personal data on a client's behalf, we act only on the client's documented instructions and under a written processing agreement. We engage subprocessors only with the client's prior written authorisation, which may be specific or general. Under a general authorisation, we tell the client about intended changes so they can object.

We transfer personal data outside the UK only where UK data protection law permits it.

Security and incidents

We protect client information with security appropriate to the risk. Access to it, including access given to AI tools, is limited to the people and systems the work needs.

If we become aware of a breach affecting client information, we act to contain it, tell the client without undue delay and help with their response. Where the breach affects personal data we control, we also make any report the law requires.

Retention

We keep working information, including copies held in AI services, only for as long as the work needs it. At the end of an engagement we return or delete it as agreed with the client. Where we act as a processor, personal data is returned or deleted at the client's choice, unless the law requires retention. Provider retention terms must be compatible with our obligations to the client.

Records we keep for legal, tax or claims purposes, such as contracts and invoices, are covered by our privacy notice.

Human review

A person at QVXX reviews work produced with AI before we rely on it or deliver it, and checks important facts, figures and code. AI tools do not publish, send messages outside QVXX or change a client's live systems unless the client has agreed how that works and a person can step in. We provide our services with reasonable care and skill.

Meetings

We tell participants before recording or transcribing a meeting, including with an AI note taker, obtain any permissions required by law and follow agreed restrictions.

Questions

Subject to applicable law, agreed client terms take precedence over this policy. To ask how we use AI with your information, email privacy@qvxx.ai.
